    How the numbers are calculated

    Distances are measured from the front rim of the cap toward the bottle opening. The CP uses the Barrowman method with the bottle diameter as the reference. The CG is the mass-weighted average of every part at the positions shown in the drawing.

    Two effects are uncertain, so they have their own settings. The cap's tapered back and the bottle shoulder both produce a backward side force in theory, but the flow separates behind them, so the real force is smaller. A factor of 1 is full Barrowman, 0 ignores it. The margin range below the main value shows the result for a cap factor from 0 to 1.

    Fin clearance compares the payload's distance from the CG with the distance of the farthest fin point from the CG. When the rocket flips about its CG at apogee, the fins sweep a circle of that radius. Above about 1.3 the payload starts clear of that circle.

    Confirm the final design with a swing test, because these are estimates.
